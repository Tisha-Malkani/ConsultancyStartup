# Supply Chain Cost-Out Playbook

This NovaConsult brief outlines a practical approach to identifying logistics and supply chain savings without weakening service levels.

## What to review

- Freight spend by lane, carrier, mode, and business unit
- Warehouse node utilization and service coverage
- OTIF, lead time, fill rate, and exception trends
- Empty miles, route density, load utilization, and detention cost
- Demand volatility and inventory buffer policies

## Recommended sequence

1. Build a baseline of cost, service, and network performance.
2. Segment lanes and SKUs by criticality, velocity, and variability.
3. Identify consolidation, routing, node, and policy opportunities.
4. Run pilots with clear owner, benefit, and risk assumptions.
5. Track realized savings through a monthly governance cadence.

## Typical outputs

- Savings opportunity heatmap
- Network redesign roadmap
- Pilot charter and KPI tree
- Executive benefit case
