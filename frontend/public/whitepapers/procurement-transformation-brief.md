# Procurement Transformation Brief

This NovaConsult brief outlines how procurement teams can move from reactive buying to strategic value creation.

## Diagnostic focus

- Spend visibility and category taxonomy
- Supplier fragmentation and concentration risk
- Contract coverage, renewal leakage, and compliance gaps
- Maverick spend and buying-channel discipline
- ESG, quality, delivery, and commercial supplier performance

## Transformation moves

1. Clean and classify spend data.
2. Build category strategies for high-value and high-risk categories.
3. Rationalize suppliers while protecting resilience.
4. Introduce scorecards, governance, and performance-linked reviews.
5. Track savings from identified opportunity to realized financial impact.

## Typical outputs

- Category opportunity map
- Supplier rationalization plan
- Negotiation and RFQ playbook
- Savings governance dashboard
