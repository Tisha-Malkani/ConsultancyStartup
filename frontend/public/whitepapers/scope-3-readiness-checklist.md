# Scope 3 Readiness Checklist

This NovaConsult brief helps operations, procurement, and sustainability teams prepare for supplier-linked emissions reporting.

## Readiness areas

- Supplier spend and material category mapping
- Emission factor selection and data-quality scoring
- Supplier questionnaire design
- Governance for data owners, validation, and exceptions
- Reduction opportunities across logistics, sourcing, packaging, and materials

## Practical steps

1. Prioritize categories that represent the majority of spend or emissions exposure.
2. Start with defensible estimates, then improve data quality over time.
3. Embed ESG metrics into supplier reviews and sourcing decisions.
4. Maintain an audit trail for assumptions, emission factors, and supplier responses.
5. Convert reporting into a commercial reduction roadmap.

## Typical outputs

- Scope 3 baseline model
- Supplier data request pack
- ESG supplier scorecard
- Reduction initiative pipeline
